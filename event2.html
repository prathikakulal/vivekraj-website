Vivek Raj Speaks on Responsible AI and Agri Transformation at India AI Impact Summit 2026

February 16, 2026
Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi
India AI Impact Summit 2026

Vivek Raj, Founder and CEO of Panama Corporation, presented a strong case for the transformation of agriculture through artificial intelligence at the India AI Impact Summit 2026 held at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi.

He was part of Panel 1 titled AI in Action — Building India's Scalable Trusted Health Innovation Ecosystem, alongside Dr Anurag Agarwal, Dr Subi Chaturvedi, Dr Kshitij Jadhav, and Umakant Soni. He also had an insightful discussion with Satish Kumar Modi, Chair of the Modi Galien Foundation India.

Vivek Raj highlighted how agriculture is moving from uncertainty to predictability through intelligence, data, and controlled environments. He noted that AI driven systems — sensors, predictive models, and automated controls — allow farms to respond in real time to plant needs, environmental shifts, and disease risks.

He also spoke about building self sustained agricultural systems integrating solar energy, biogas, and in house AI infrastructure, reducing dependence on external supply chains. He raised the limited focus of CSR initiatives on agriculture and called for enabling intelligent farming systems as a national priority.
